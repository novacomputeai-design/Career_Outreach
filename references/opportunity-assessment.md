# Opportunity-first assessment

This is the agent-independent research contract for comparing personal career opportunities. Apply it before researching people. It does not grant contact, application, novelty, or outreach readiness.

## Opportunity types

- **DIRECT_HIRING:** A specific advertised role. Record the exact title and requisition when available, source and observation date, work location, application status evidence, actual responsibilities, mandatory qualifications, and relevant reporting or operating context. Distinguish currently accepting, status unverified, closed, and stale.
- **STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION:** A source-supported organizational initiative without a verified advertised role. State that hiring is unconfirmed. Describe one bounded leadership contribution that complements named incumbents, the decision or outcome it could support, and the evidence behind the hypothesis. A general AI announcement alone is insufficient.

## Candidate opportunity record

For each opportunity, capture:

1. Organization, opportunity type, exact role or initiative, location/work mode, source URLs, publication dates, and date observed.
2. The documented organizational need, its timing, and evidence strength.
3. For a job: the actual core responsibilities and mandatory requirements; map each important requirement to supplied candidate evidence as `SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL`, `NOT_EVIDENCED`, or `NOT_MET` when evidence establishes a miss. Preserve unknowns. Do not infer achievements, eligibility, or qualifications.
4. For strategic exploration: the proposed bounded contribution, why it is distinct from current leaders' remit, a relevant conversation question, and an observable business outcome.
5. Leadership scope and career potential: decision ownership, portfolio/team scope, cross-functional exposure, and plausible growth. Label hypotheses as hypotheses.
6. Unresolved matters such as vacancy status, required experience, location feasibility, budget or mandate, and source freshness.

## Compare and rank before people research

For DIRECT_HIRING, report two related but distinct judgments: **opportunity quality** (organizational need, role scope, timing, and career potential) and **candidate feasibility** (actual match to mandatory qualifications, work authorization/location eligibility when evidenced, and work-mode feasibility). Mandatory requirements and actual eligibility affect feasibility and the candidate-opportunity ranking. Map each mandatory requirement to evidence; a missing fact is UNVERIFIED, while an established unmet requirement is NOT_MET. Do not call either one proof the organization has no worthwhile need. Keep optional/preferred requirements distinct from mandatory requirements. For STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION, assess fit to the evidenced need and bounded contribution, while explicitly marking hiring and eligibility as unconfirmed.

Compare opportunities on candidate fit and feasibility, evidenced organizational need and timing, leadership contribution and scope, career potential, location/work-mode feasibility, and evidence quality. Explain the most consequential trade-offs in plain language. Do not use a numerical opportunity score unless the user asks for one; do not manufacture precision or force a quota.

When an organization has multiple relevant roles or initiatives, compare them side by side before choosing that organization's strongest lead. Record the alternatives considered and why each ranks higher or lower. Do not let a senior title outrank a better actual fit by default.

Keep organizational opportunity quality and its underlying need independent from:

- whether a suitable executive has been identified;
- whether a person-specific public route exists;
- private relationship-history or novelty status;
- contact readiness, outreach approval, or contact authorization.

Candidate feasibility is a separate ranking dimension for DIRECT_HIRING and can lower the candidate's ranking for a particular role when mandatory qualifications or eligibility are unmet or unverified. It must not be obscured by a strong title, attractive need, contact route, or relationship history. Record the role's opportunity quality even when candidate feasibility is low or unknown.

These are separate downstream findings. A strong research opportunity remains reportable when any of them is unknown or on hold. Such a hold must still block the corresponding contact-ready or authorization state under the existing policy.

## Handoff

After an initial, explicitly provisional ranking, research relevant leaders and process contacts for selected opportunities. Pass the exact opportunity record and intended conversation or hiring-process purpose to leadership discovery. Leadership research may revise the opportunity ranking when it yields new, sourced facts about organizational needs, timing, incumbents, responsibilities, authority, role scope, or candidate feasibility. Record the prior rank, new fact and source, reason for the change, and revised rank. Mere accessibility, contact-route availability, recruiter responsiveness, or relationship novelty must never revise opportunity ranking. Attach people, authority claims, and routes to that opportunity; do not rank people across unrelated opportunities as a substitute for ranking the opportunities themselves.
