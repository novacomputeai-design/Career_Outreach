# Personal Career Intelligence V2.2 — Draft Integrated Operating Contract

**DRAFT / NOT ACTIVE FOR PRODUCTION.** This document extends the V2.1 safety, privacy, project-scope and human-governance controls; it never relaxes them. The V2.1 evaluator is reused as a non-contact preflight, and the V2.2 layer may only downgrade research readiness, not grant external-action authority.

## 1. Goal and search order

Candidate aims at permanent **internal senior leadership employment**. Industry-agnostic; Tucson/Southern Arizona first, feasible U.S.-remote second. AI strategy/transformation (60) and enterprise product leadership (40) are research emphases across CAPABILITY THEMES, never search quotas or pathway allocations. The two valid independent pathways remain DIRECT_HIRING and STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION.

For strategic search, begin with **evidenced business change**. Research: objective -> initiative/maturity -> bounded business decision -> complementary INTERNAL leadership responsibility -> candidate career evidence -> ownership map -> decision-maker -> specific timing trigger -> legitimate access route -> owner review. Never begin with seniority or AI keyword count alone. For direct hiring, independently verify current primary ATS role, employment type, location, mandatory candidate match, and current application status. The same organization may have multiple opportunities.

## 2. Versioned contribution assessment

All V2.2 opportunities contain \`career_value\`:

- \`business_decision\`: specific organizational decision or adoption problem; label inferred if not explicitly documented
- \`proposed_internal_responsibility\`: bounded possible leadership scope, NOT an invented vacancy
- \`candidate_evidence_refs\`: IDs from approved public career profile only (Mobius product portfolio, AI prioritization, product team leadership, UChicago modernization, Wells Fargo regulated operations)
- \`complementary_scope\`: work this leader could direct **alongside** the existing AI/CIO/technical team; not an assertion current leaders are inadequate
- \`existing_owner_status\`: EXISTING_OWNER_DOCUMENTED | OWNER_RESEARCHED_NOT_FOUND | OWNER_UNKNOWN; not found does NOT mean nonexistent
- \`expected_outcome\`, \`limitations\`: specific proposed value and candidate constraints
- \`trigger_claim_id\`: source-claimed BUSINESS_TRIGGER or null
- \`maturity\`: ANNOUNCED | PILOTED | DEPLOYED | SCALING | UNKNOWN

Qualities: STRONG_CONTRIBUTION | CONTRIBUTION_RESEARCH_NEEDED | NO_CREDIBLE_CONTRIBUTION. Timing: RESEARCH_NOW | MONITOR_TRIGGER | NO_TIMING_BASIS; RESEARCH_NOW means research a timely business trigger and **never** send a message. The assessor checks field structure, allowed résumé-reference identifiers, source claimed business-objective linkage and dated trigger but cannot judge truth of an actual internal staffing gap, nor clinical/utility qualifications. Source truth remains UNVERIFIED unless separately evidenced.

## 3. Source, executive, and route truth

A source URL, exact string excerpt, \`page_read_success\` flag, employer press release or online biography supplied by an agent is **not independently verified**. Independent witness source/owner checks must be separately captured before treating claim as current verified fact. If host cannot attest, mark NOT_VERIFIED and HOLD on outreach. Search contradictory executive affiliation sources and bind every current-role claim to person AND employer. DOCUMENTED initiative ownership requires evidence explicitly linking PERSON to INITIATIVE; CIO/CAIO title is functionally plausible, not proof.

Access route states are PROFILE_IDENTIFIED / PERSON_SPECIFIC_MANUAL_ROUTE_VERIFIED / WARM_INTRO_VERIFIED / INDIRECT_GENERAL / NO_VERIFIED_PERSON_ROUTE. LinkedIn profile identification alone does not prove message availability or consent; general press inbox and switchboard are not individual access. Owner-only privacy screening remains required before any contemplated contact. Only the trusted operator side may access DPAPI. No private keys, history, feedback or personal email in public repo.

## 4. Safe staging and handoff

- **Codex:** source research, specific business decisions, bounded career contribution, public executive/route mapping, explicit self-reported source/skill limits. Native skill proof must come from the HOST, not self-written traces.
- **V2.2 offline Python:** strict schema, structural evaluator, career quality/timing check, immutable private PUBLIC-RESEARCH-ONLY opportunity run snapshots; never independently claims live source truth or sends.
- **Trusted owner Windows environment:** private-memory classification, owner feedback audit and recovery **only with explicit authorization** and qualified trusted path. Research runner cannot invoke this step or infer novelty.
- **Owner review:** PURSUE / RESEARCH_MORE / REJECT with reasons, not a source-authentication or contact-approval mechanism.
- **Gate A/B:** exact draft and later exact send authorization are distinct; current send state BLOCKED. Applications require a separate owner action and never auto-submit.

A successful V2.2 packet reports stage_manifest values truthfully. \`source_truth_independently_verified=false\`, \`native_skills_verified=false\` and \`contact_ready=0\` are intentionally fixed in the offline preflight.

## 5. Continuity and learning

The V2.2 private snapshot ledger is per-run append only, outside Git and separate from the DPAPI history. It remembers each (employer, ATS requisition) and (employer, strategic initiative, mandate) key across runs. NOT_OBSERVED_THIS_RUN != CLOSED, INACTIVE, REJECTED or no longer worthwhile. Never merge same-employer distinct postings.

Owner decision/outcome events require a **separately protected, authenticated owner-controlled store**; the public-only ledger rejects owner decisions. Advisory feedback learning may propose changes only on owner-supplied, independently authenticated inputs; it cannot apply preferences, change score thresholds, alter hard gates, or send.
