# Daily V2.1 organizational opportunity discovery contract

**Authority:** `config/operating-policy.json > career_intelligence` and `references/career-intelligence-operating-contract-v2.1.md`. Branch is a candidate only.

## Objective
Discover worthwhile, evidence-backed paths to permanent internal AI strategy/transformation or complex enterprise product leadership. Tucson/Southern Arizona first; suitable US remote; any industry. The 60/40 emphasis is by capability theme and directional; STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION and DIRECT_HIRING are independent engagement pathways. No numeric-score or 20-contact quota promotion.

## Workflow
1. Research organizations and exact initiatives, business objectives, execution and hiring evidence. Cite visited first-party claims, publisher, origin, date, excerpt and conflicts. Unknown maturity does not imply absence.
2. Build bounded resume-linked contribution and separate fact from potential unmet leadership challenge HYPOTHESIS.
3. Map actual initiative sponsor, business outcome owner, AI/tech executor, product owner, hiring sponsor and introducer. DOCUMENTED requires an exact person-to-initiative claim; current title is separate evidence.
4. Strategic ASSESSABLE findings can have unknown owners; targeted engagement needs documented owner and person-specific permitted route. Direct hiring with independently checked current ATS, mandatory fit and feasible work mode survives without a named manager. Application is owner-controlled manual action only.
5. Verify person routes and owner-only private relationship history only when individual contact is contemplated; old contacts never block new initiatives. Respect person opt-outs without erasing an independent verified vacancy.
6. Audit with reason-coded ASSESSABLE, RESEARCH_PENDING, EVIDENCE_HOLD, CLOSED_INACTIVE or EXCLUDED, separate owner Pursue/Research more/Reject and separate engagement readiness. Report honest research gaps and zero useful findings if appropriate.
7. Skill activation is conditional; log actual native host selection or NOT_VERIFIED; SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE is valid when documented. No substituting legacy Python execution as Codex-native evidence.

## Readiness and actions
owner_decision is preference, not source proof, Gate A content approval, Gate B permission or a job application. software SEND_AUTHORIZED is BLOCKED. Contact-ready, sent, booked, applied, interviewed and hired remain false absent independently verified human events. Public Git and current person-only 19-column exporter never store private notes, keys or contact history. V2 opportunity records use separate versioned reporting.
