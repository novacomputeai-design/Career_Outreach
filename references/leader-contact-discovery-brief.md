# Leader and professional contact discovery — human-facing brief contract

**Purpose:** A compact read-only Codex shortlist of the best people to contact
about the owner's *personal* AI strategy/transformation or enterprise product
leadership career goals. This is NOT a new agent, database schema, application
or sending workflow. Existing V2.1/V2.2 safety and source-truth limits govern.

## One result per useful leader

For each organization, report:
- **Organization and work feasibility:** Tucson/Southern Arizona first, genuinely feasible US-remote next; industry agnostic.
- **Why now:** one concrete, dated business initiative / product change / official job with an exact visited source. Distinguish observed employer claim, inferred opportunity, and confirmed opening.
- **Primary leader:** current full name, exact employer and title, separately sourced current-role evidence; resolve departures and namesake confusion.
- **Why this leader:** DOCUMENTED link to the business outcome or initiative, or clearly marked FUNCTIONALLY_PLAUSIBLE. Explain decision influence and why this person is more appropriate/accessible than the CEO. Give up to one evidence-supported alternate, not a second generic executive.
- **Why this candidate:** one bounded responsibility related to the approved public résumé, a concrete value hypothesis, and limitations. Hiring interest must not be inferred from AI mentions.
- **Professional contact:** type, exact public work contact value *if listed*, publisher and source URL, last checked date, contact-to-person linkage and any accessibility caveat. Use NOT_FOUND rather than guessed or personal details.
- **Next manual step:** e.g. inspect official directory, check a newer role source, request owner review of a sourced contact. No unsolicited sending or automatic application.

## Organization-first search and repeat transparency

Before selecting named executives, review an **owner-provided, public-safe prior
research list** if available. It is a bounded research-exclusion aid, not a
private relationship or outreach-history lookup. Never inspect DPAPI, private
SQLite, contact notes, or prior-owner relationship data from Codex.

For a focused five-leader test, screen approximately **5–8 distinct employers**
with credible public business triggers, then **deeply investigate the best
three organizations** before expanding. Record current roles, initiative
ownership, relevant alternatives, and the source paths checked for published
person-specific work contacts. This is a research-depth guide, not a quota;
report actual coverage and shortfalls. Do not start with familiar executive
names or invent a business justification.

For each displayed record show exactly one **public research reuse status**:

- `NEW_IN_THIS_RESEARCH`: not in the supplied public prior-research list and
  discovered from an employer-first source. **Not proof** the owner has never
  encountered or contacted the person.
- `ALREADY_RESEARCHED`: same named leader or same employer opportunity was
  previously surfaced; separate from fresh leads.
- `UPDATED_OPPORTUNITY`: a genuinely distinct, newly evidenced initiative or
  requisition at an already researched employer; does **not** make that
  employer or previously known person new.
- `REPEAT_STATUS_UNKNOWN`: no adequate public prior-research list to compare.

Always mark **private relationship novelty UNVERIFIED** unless the owner
separately supplies a safe, authenticated minimal classification outside Codex.
Do not describe a new-to-public-research result as a newly verified contact.

For the **two-sentence conversation thesis**, cite the specific employer
business decision, name the limited internal leadership contribution that
matches approved candidate history, and note whether the relevant AI/product
executive's remit already covers it. When responsibility might simply duplicate
that chief's job, return `CONTRIBUTION_UNCLEAR` and a next research question,
not a generic invitation to discuss "AI strategy." Never imply hiring or a
budget without source evidence.

## Preserve promising people when a route or work mode is unknown

Return two distinct sections: **PERSON_ROUTE_SUPPORTED** for cited person-specific
professional routes, and **PROMISING_RESEARCH_PROSPECTS** for credible current
leaders with a source-backed business trigger and bounded contribution thesis
but only PROFILE_ONLY / CONTACT_HOLD route evidence. The latter must show
what official directory, public event, and individual profile sources were
checked and one next evidence step; they are not contact-ready or eligible
for sending.

Evaluate **professional-conversation relevance** separately from whether a
future position can be performed in Tucson or remotely. An unknown or
infeasible employment work mode is a material hiring-path limitation, but
does not erase an otherwise relevant strategic networking lead. Label
employment feasibility UNKNOWN / INFEASIBLE where supported. An official
current role without an identified hiring manager stays an employer hiring
opportunity, not a fabricated executive contact.

## Repeat baseline and ranked leader selection

Read `config/public-career-research-baseline.json` before starting discovery.
It contains only names/employers from publicly discussed research, never
personal relationships, conversation notes or contact addresses. Compare
**person plus employer** for people and separately check employer aliases.
Do not mislabel an indexed person or employer as a fresh discovery; do
not suppress a genuinely distinct new initiative at a known employer.
When the index is missing or partial, mark repeat status UNKNOWN. A
new-to-research leader is **not** proven new to the owner. Do not mutate the
baseline automatically; propose public-only additions for owner review.

For each shortlisted employer, compare up to three relevant leaders:
outcome owner, product/transformation VP or director, and enterprise AI/CIO
leader. Prefer directly connected responsibility, credible distinct
contribution and realistic professional access; seniority is last. Explain
why the chosen primary is preferable to a plausible alternate or CEO,
without upgrading a functional inference to documented ownership.

The conversation thesis must describe the specific, sourced business
change; an affected workflow, product or user; a concrete decision and
one bounded internal leadership contribution linked to approved experience;
the plausible metric; and existing-owner overlap. Separate all inference
from employer facts. `CONTRIBUTION_UNCLEAR` is valid: a broad AI
announcement is not evidence of an executive vacancy or a missing team.

## Contact-search completion and current-use interpretation

For each primary/alternate, try at least three independent public-source
lanes when available unless a **current, suitable named work route** is
already confirmed: (1) official employer directory/biography,
(2) current professional event or association sources, (3) lawfully
public institutional filings or government/utility/contract PDFs,
and (4) an individual identity-checked profile as fallback.
Keep a one-line search trail per leader; stop honestly if a lane has no
accessible sources. Never use a restricted page, scraping, predicted
email pattern, personal number, or automated outreach.

A contract's named work contact can be displayed as historical
professional evidence, **not automatically a suitable networking route**.
Record contact source date, identity, employer role at the time, later
role verification and purpose restriction separately:

- `CURRENT_PERSON_WORK_ROUTE`: source-linked individual professional
  contact and appropriate current business context, no unresolved conflict.
  May map to `PERSON_ROUTE_SUPPORTED` as a *research* finding only.
- `HISTORICAL_PERSON_WORK_CONTACT_RECHECK`: archived/old source; show
  date and value in private research brief but hold current-use claim.
- `PURPOSE_RESTRICTED_ROUTE_REVIEW`: contact given specifically for
  procurement, utility contract administration, legal or media purposes;
  not assumed suitable for a job-networking approach.
- `PROFILE_ONLY`, `INDIRECT_GENERAL`, `NO_VERIFIED_PERSON_ROUTE`:
  no source-supported appropriate direct work route.

Historical or purpose-limited findings belong under
`PROMISING_RESEARCH_PROSPECTS` or `CONTACT_HOLD`, *not* the count of
currently suitable named routes. A source-listed professional contact
never establishes message delivery, relationship history, recipient
interest, or SEND_AUTHORIZED; sending remains blocked.

## Practical Tucson-first selection and contact-stop rule

For a Tucson-first diagnostic, require evidence for **both** an employer's local
operation and the **specific initiative's** local ownership/impact before
ranking it as a Tucson opportunity. A Tempe expansion plus a Tucson office is
`LOCAL_INITIATIVE_UNVERIFIED`, not a Tucson project. Preserve a separate
verified US-remote pathway when permitted, with Arizona eligibility
evidenced rather than assumed. Never manufacture a third deep employer when
only two have local initiative support.

Keep **ACCOUNTABLE_SPONSOR** distinct from **FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET**.
The former may be a COO/CEO/CIO with the clearest initiative connection;
the latter may be a more accessible, source-verified operating VP or product
Director with genuine business decision proximity. Only select the latter
as the practical primary when **current affiliation, relevant functional
connection, and a legitimate contact pathway** are supported. If an older
event names a VP but fresh current-role evidence is missing, mark
`FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET_CANDIDATE — RESEARCH_HOLD`. Do not promote a
plausible VP to a DOCUMENTED sponsor or substitute an unrelated accessible
person for the rightful owner.

For a primary and at most one relevant alternate, perform a bounded pass
through the accessible official employer, professional event/publication,
and public institutional-document search lanes, plus at most one targeted
recheck; log actual query/source paths and failures. A current suitable
person-specific route can end the search early. If no suitable route is
supported, mark `CONTACT_SEARCH_COMPLETED_NO_SUITABLE_ROUTE`, preserve
the lead as a research prospect, and investigate the next **already-screened
qualified employer** rather than looping indefinitely or inventing an email.
This finding means *not found in the bounded sources*, not *no route exists*.

A conversation-worthy thesis must identify an initiative-specific business
decision, affected workflow, **one distinct bounded internal leadership
responsibility**, a plausible metric, and known incumbent overlap. A generic
portfolio-prioritization proposal which merely restates the incumbent CIO's
or COO's remit is `CONTRIBUTION_UNCLEAR`. Keep factual opportunity
research separate from hiring demand, contact readiness and authorization.

## Contact types and report-only result buckets

| Contact type | Evidence required | Brief bucket |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LISTED_WORK_EMAIL | Source expressly pairs named current professional with their published employer/work address | PERSON_ROUTE_SUPPORTED |
| LISTED_OFFICE_PHONE | Official/current source expressly pairs named person with an office number; not main switchboard | PERSON_ROUTE_SUPPORTED |
| VERIFIED_PERSON_SPECIFIC_FORM | Official permitted public professional form that targets named person, source-linked | PERSON_ROUTE_SUPPORTED |
| INDIVIDUAL_PROFILE_IDENTIFIED | Identity-checked profile; messaging reachability/permission NOT established | PROFILE_ONLY |
| INDIRECT_GENERAL | General company line, HR/press inbox or generic contact form only | CONTACT_HOLD |
| NONE | No trustworthy, current person-specific route | CONTACT_HOLD |

A published work contact is source **listed**, not independently confirmed to
deliver a message. A person-specific source contact does not establish an
existing relationship, acceptance of contact, hiring interest, or readiness
to send. These three buckets are HUMAN-FACING FINDING labels only and MUST
NOT replace `research_state`, `engagement_readiness`, `contact_ready`,
`send_authorized`, or protected owner history. The system state `SEND_AUTHORIZED` remains BLOCKED.

## Brief presentation

A compact table may summarize:
`Organization | Why now (source) | Leader/title (role source) | Why this leader | Work contact/type (source, checked) | Bucket | Next step`.

For a leadership-hiring route with a current official ATS but no safe person,
retain that official opening in a separate employer-opportunity backlog with
`EXECUTIVE_OWNER_UNVERIFIED` and **no invented hiring manager**. Do not
count it as a verified person contact.

Totals should differentiate investigated organizations, current named
leaders, DOCUMENTED vs FUNCTIONALLY_PLAUSIBLE responsibility, sourced
person-specific contact values, profile-only, and contact holds. It is fine
to return fewer than the requested count.

## Working with the V2.2 packet schema

The existing strict research **input** schema has
`executive.route = {route_type, verified_for_person, url}`; it does not
store an email address or telephone number. Do not silently jam contact
values into the `url` field or expand the schema without a separate,
tested change. The human-facing report may present published professional
email/phone as a **sourced display-only contact finding**, saved to the
owner-approved private research output, outside PUBLIC Git.

When a DIRECT route has evidence, `executive.route.url` refers to its
public HTTPS source/official route page, and only the human-facing brief
shows the listed email/office phone. A mere URL to a generic bio/contact
page cannot set `verified_for_person=true`. An identified LinkedIn URL
is at most PROFILE_ONLY until a permitted person-specific messaging route
is actually established. No automated LinkedIn interaction or scraping.

**Always:** no invented addresses, scraped personal numbers, private emails,
credential bypass, false current-affiliation claims, contacts in PUBLIC Git,
DPAPI access from Codex, drafting without request, sending or applications.
Native skill activation remains NOT_VERIFIED absent independent host proof.
