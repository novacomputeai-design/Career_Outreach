# Provisional evidence-based person score
First rank the underlying opportunity using `opportunity-assessment.md`. This rubric scores a person-to-person career connection only; it must not be used to rank, suppress, or rescue an opportunity. These weights are hypotheses for calibration, not predictive confidence.

| Factor | Maximum | How to substantiate |
|---|---:|---|
| Transferable leadership/capability fit | 30 | Resume evidence + organization's documented need/initiative |
| Decision influence or introductions | 25 | Actual position and documented scope; don't equate title with hiring authority |
| Authentic specific conversation reason | 20 | Concrete topical common interest anchored to verifiable fact |
| Professional reachability | 15 | Verified public direct/indirect/warm/manual route, correctly labeled |
| Work geography / mode | 10 | Tucson preferred; fully remote US qualifies |

>=85 strong; 70–84 qualified; 50–69 research further; <50 reject for the person-to-person connection only. Mandatory hard gates override total for contact readiness: verified name/current title/org/source; individualized reason; legitimate route; novelty check; no prior suppression; >=70. A low or unavailable person score, missing route, or unresolved history must not lower the separate opportunity ranking. When evidence is insufficient HOLD the affected person/contact state; preserve a strong research opportunity. Do not silently raise weights to reach 20/day.
