## Procedure

Use the V2.1 opportunity record. Assess independently: organizational attractiveness; demonstrated candidate contribution (objective -> decision/problem HYPOTHESIS -> approved experience -> plausible value -> limits); and feasible leadership-employment/engagement path. Mark mismatched mandatory credentials and infeasible nonremote work location as holds, not low scores. Treat geography as search priority, not a substitute for value.

STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION: a directly evidenced strategic signal and truthful contribution may be ASSESSABLE without a known sponsor, but never assumed hiring. Only DOCUMENTED relevant executive ownership plus specific verified route yields ENGAGEMENT_REVIEW_ELIGIBLE (still not contact-ready). DIRECT_HIRING: verify official active ATS requisition, mandatory fit, Arizona/remote feasibility; retain valid role without manager; manual application requires owner authorization.

## Pathway-specific candidate fit — do not cross-apply the gates

**DIRECT_HIRING:** The advertised position is the leadership responsibility being evaluated. Compare the approved public candidate profile against the **actual advertised work, mandatory years/credentials, leadership experience and Arizona/Tucson or remote eligibility**. A current official opening may remain a `PROMISING_RESEARCH_ONLY` opportunity while ATS acceptance, work location, a required credential or the hiring owner is unresolved. Preserve the official application path separately; it does not authorize applying or messaging. **Do not require a second, complementary internal leadership role beyond the advertised vacancy, and do not mark `CONTRIBUTION_UNCLEAR` merely because the advertised work overlaps the CTO/CDO's department.** A genuine mandatory-qualification mismatch is a fit HOLD or LOW_CAREER_FIT, not automatically a rejection without checking the resume evidence.

**STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION:** No vacancy is evidenced; test whether a real initiative creates a *plausible distinct permanent internal leadership responsibility* not simply duplicating the incumbent's present remit. In focused leader discovery, add a **two-sentence specific conversation thesis**: (1) sourced business change and affected users/workflow or product with one initiative-specific decision/adoption problem; (2) a bounded INTERNAL contribution grounded in approved candidate evidence, measurable possible outcome and the incumbent owner/overlap. Mark inferred challenges HYPOTHESIS. Ask **Why this person, why this contribution, why now?** No AI initiative proves an unfilled role or hiring intent.

**Reject generic strategic value propositions:** For strategic exploration, specify one initiative-specific decision, bounded responsibility, testable measure, current incumbent owner and what remains unknown. If no defensible complementary scope emerges, label `CONTRIBUTION_UNCLEAR` and retain a research prospect, not a conversation-ready lead. For DIRECT_HIRING, a concise candidate-to-advertised-role thesis replaces this hypothetical gap-finding exercise. Both pathways require credible user benefit and source-grounded fit, not recycled generic AI/product slogans.

Never let old >=70/75 person/organization scores override a missing claim, owner rejection, route, credentials, opt-out or other hard gate. Return research_state, engagement_readiness, reasons and blocked next actions.

## Conversation-worthiness before contact convenience

Do not confuse **interesting company**, **reachable person**, and **credible personal leadership opportunity**. Evaluate each independently. For **STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION**, a prospect is `CONVERSATION_WORTH_EXPLORING` only when the source-backed initiative has a consequential business decision or implementation gap to *investigate*, the named leader is relevant to that decision, the candidate's proven skills could support a genuinely **distinct, bounded internal leadership responsibility**, and a specific timely question makes a peer conversation credible. For **DIRECT_HIRING**, judge the advertised role and evidence-based candidate fit on their own terms; do not require a distinct second role. Neither label certifies a current opening, suitable contact or owner approval. A plausible hypothesis is allowed; do not claim a staffing gap, available role, recruiting intent or organizational deficiency without evidence.

Use `PROMISING_RESEARCH_ONLY` if the business problem is promising but sponsor, current implementation status or distinct responsibility is unresolved. Use `LOW_CAREER_FIT` when the opportunity's evidenced scope is mainly production staffing, product sales, executive duties already fully owned or otherwise unrelated to the candidate's permanent internal leadership objective—even if a contact email is easily found. These are **human review labels**, not new machine states or score thresholds. The contact route cannot improve the factual contribution score or override a weak fit. For STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION, before promoting a conversation thesis, explicitly answer: **What would I ask this leader about their business decision, and what could I personally own that they don't already clearly own?** If this cannot be answered without a generic pitch, hold. For DIRECT_HIRING, ask instead: **How does my documented experience match this exact advertised responsibility, and what requirement still needs verification?**

## Evidence-based leadership demand triage

After establishing the business initiative, explicitly ask **why an employer might need a distinct senior internal product/AI/transformation leader now**. Ground the answer in a current official leadership vacancy (`VERIFIED_LEADERSHIP_OPENING`), a source-backed change in organizational remit or business implementation mandate (`STRATEGIC_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL`), or say `INITIATIVE_ONLY_NO_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL`. The middle case is *research justification only*: assigning work to an incumbent can mean the organization is already staffed, and a launch announcement by itself says nothing about open leadership needs. A present vacancy still requires location/mandatory-qualification fit; a strategy conversation still requires a genuinely distinct bounded role hypothesis, not a claim of hiring intent.

Rank candidate opportunities by **evidence of leadership demand plus candidate-to-role match for DIRECT_HIRING or distinct transferable responsibility for STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION**, then Tucson/eligible-remote feasibility, then accountable decision-maker relevance, and only later contact convenience. If all three precommitted pilot employers lack evidence, report **zero** qualified leadership conversations and the next specific discovery question. Do not substitute volume, executive prestige, company size, published email or a generic AI proposal for fit.

## Output boundary

Return only cited facts, clearly marked hypotheses and reason-coded HOLDs. Record whether this specialist was natively selected, NOT_VERIFIED, or SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE. Research findings and drafts are not contact-ready or sent.
