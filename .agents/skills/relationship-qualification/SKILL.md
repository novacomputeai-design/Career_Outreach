# relationship-qualification

## Procedure

Use the V2.1 opportunity record. Assess independently: organizational attractiveness; demonstrated candidate contribution (objective -> decision/problem HYPOTHESIS -> approved experience -> plausible value -> limits); and feasible leadership-employment/engagement path. Mark mismatched mandatory credentials and infeasible nonremote work location as holds, not low scores. Treat geography as search priority, not a substitute for value.

STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION: a directly evidenced strategic signal and truthful contribution may be ASSESSABLE without a known sponsor, but never assumed hiring. Only DOCUMENTED relevant executive ownership plus specific verified route yields ENGAGEMENT_REVIEW_ELIGIBLE (still not contact-ready). DIRECT_HIRING: verify official active ATS requisition, mandatory fit, Arizona/remote feasibility; retain valid role without manager; manual application requires owner authorization.

In focused leader discovery, add a **two-sentence specific conversation thesis** before promoting a person to the human-facing shortlist: (1) the sourced business change and a concrete decision/adoption/product problem; (2) a bounded possible INTERNAL leadership responsibility grounded in the approved candidate profile, plus how it would complement rather than replace the known CIO/CAIO/product/transformation remit. Separate employer-stated facts from the candidate-fit hypothesis. If a senior AI leader already owns the work and there is no evidence of a distinct useful role, mark CONTRIBUTION_UNCLEAR and keep the contact as research-only; do not pitch a generic AI strategy or imply hiring. An evidence-supported new initiative at an already known employer may still be valuable but is not a new leader discovery.

**Reject generic value propositions:** Before a leader becomes a primary research prospect, the conversation thesis must identify (a) a cited business change and affected users/workflow or product, (b) an actual decision or adoption challenge (if inferred, label HYPOTHESIS), (c) one **bounded** internal responsibility grounded in the approved candidate evidence, (d) a plausible observable outcome or metric, and (e) incumbent-owner overlap and what remains unknown. Two concise sentences are the target; don't repeat the same "AI strategy" or "product portfolio method" for unrelated employers. Explicitly answer: *Why this person, why this contribution, why now?* No source proves a staffing gap merely because an initiative exists. When the specific contribution is unsupported, return CONTRIBUTION_UNCLEAR and keep a clearly marked research prospect instead of inventing a role.

**Contribution distinctness check:** For each proposed person, name **one initiative-specific decision** (not "AI strategy" generally), **one bounded internal responsibility** the candidate might own (not the entire program), **one testable measure**, and the **known incumbent owner**. Answer: "What work could the candidate do that is not simply the current leader's existing job?" When no defensible complementary scope emerges from cited sources, label `CONTRIBUTION_UNCLEAR`; retain only as research rather than promoting a conversation-worthy lead. Prefer precise cases such as experiment-to-deployment adoption or manufacturing workflow quality over generic portfolio-prioritization language reused across unrelated companies. Employment feasibility and ability to hold a professional conversation remain separate judgments, and neither may be fabricated.

Never let old >=70/75 person/organization scores override a missing claim, owner rejection, route, credentials, opt-out or other hard gate. Return research_state, engagement_readiness, reasons and blocked next actions.

## Conversation-worthiness before contact convenience

Do not confuse **interesting company**, **reachable person**, and **credible personal leadership opportunity**. Evaluate each independently. A prospect is `CONVERSATION_WORTH_EXPLORING` only when the source-backed initiative has a consequential business decision or implementation gap to *investigate*, the named leader is relevant to that decision, the candidate's proven skills could support a genuinely **distinct, bounded internal leadership responsibility**, and a specific timely question makes a peer conversation credible. A plausible hypothesis is allowed; do not claim a staffing gap, available role, recruiting intent or organizational deficiency without evidence.

Use `PROMISING_RESEARCH_ONLY` if the business problem is promising but sponsor, current implementation status or distinct responsibility is unresolved. Use `LOW_CAREER_FIT` when the opportunity's evidenced scope is mainly production staffing, product sales, executive duties already fully owned or otherwise unrelated to the candidate's permanent internal leadership objective—even if a contact email is easily found. These are **human review labels**, not new machine states or score thresholds. The contact route cannot improve the factual contribution score or override a weak fit. Before promoting a conversation thesis, explicitly answer: **What would I ask this leader about their business decision, and what could I personally own that they don't already clearly own?** If this cannot be answered without a generic pitch, hold.

## Evidence-based leadership demand triage

After establishing the business initiative, explicitly ask **why an employer might need a distinct senior internal product/AI/transformation leader now**. Ground the answer in a current official leadership vacancy (`VERIFIED_LEADERSHIP_OPENING`), a source-backed change in organizational remit or business implementation mandate (`STRATEGIC_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL`), or say `INITIATIVE_ONLY_NO_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL`. The middle case is *research justification only*: assigning work to an incumbent can mean the organization is already staffed, and a launch announcement by itself says nothing about open leadership needs. A present vacancy still requires location/mandatory-qualification fit; a strategy conversation still requires a genuinely distinct bounded role hypothesis, not a claim of hiring intent.

Rank candidate opportunities by **evidence of leadership demand and distinct transferable responsibility**, then Tucson/eligible-remote feasibility, then accountable decision-maker relevance, and only later contact convenience. If all three precommitted pilot employers lack evidence, report **zero** qualified leadership conversations and the next specific discovery question. Do not substitute volume, executive prestige, company size, published email or a generic AI proposal for fit.

## Output boundary

Return only cited facts, clearly marked hypotheses and reason-coded HOLDs. Record whether this specialist was natively selected, NOT_VERIFIED, or SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE. Research findings and drafts are not contact-ready or sent.
