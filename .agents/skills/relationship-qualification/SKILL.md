# relationship-qualification

Read `../../../references/opportunity-assessment.md` and `../../../references/scoring-rubric.md`. Opportunity research and provisional ranking must be complete before scoring people. The existing 0–100 score applies only to an individual connection; it must not determine or alter opportunity rank. Allocate points out of 30 fit, 25 influence, 20 specific conversation rationale, 15 reachability, 10 location/work mode. Include precise fact URLs and distinguish evidence from hypotheses. The >=70 threshold and hard gates apply only to relationship qualification/contact readiness. A missing person-specific route, unverified novelty, or unresolved approval does not lower organizational opportunity quality, candidate feasibility, or erase a promising research-only opportunity. Don't inflate scores to meet 20. Tucson preferred but high-quality fully remote US accepted, all industries eligible. Return the person score and PASS/HOLD/REJECT separately from the opportunity finding.

## Supervised opportunity pathway assessment — avoid cross-applied requirements

For **DIRECT_HIRING**, the posted position itself is the potential leadership responsibility. Compare the approved candidate profile with the *actual advertised* duties, mandatory experience, credentials, portfolio/people-management scope, and Arizona/Tucson or US-remote feasibility. Map each mandatory requirement individually to supplied profile evidence as SUPPORTED, PARTIAL, NOT_EVIDENCED, or NOT_MET when evidence establishes it is unmet. A mandatory qualification gap or actual ineligibility lowers candidate feasibility and the candidate-opportunity ranking; it does not erase the underlying organizational opportunity. Distinguish a known unmet requirement from a missing fact. **Do not require an additional hypothetical leadership role beyond the job**, or mark `CONTRIBUTION_UNCLEAR` merely because the open position belongs to an existing executive's department. Keep qualification, ATS acceptance, geography, and named hiring authority as separately labeled findings; do not invent resume experience, a live vacancy, or applicant eligibility.

For **STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION**, no advertised position is proven. Require a source-backed business initiative and a plausible **distinct, bounded internal leadership contribution** that would complement known incumbents rather than duplicate their entire remit. Explain the decision, specific conversation question, possible business measure and remaining hiring uncertainty. A vague "AI strategy" pitch is insufficient. Do not use a publicly accessible executive to rescue an unrelated opportunity.

For either pathway, preserve valuable research-only opportunities and people even when direct contact verification, private novelty, or application status is unresolved. Leadership research may revise a provisional opportunity ranking only when new sourced facts materially change organizational need, timing, incumbents, responsibilities/authority, scope, or candidate feasibility; preserve the previous assessment and explain the evidence-linked change. Contact route availability, ease of access, recruiter responsiveness, and relationship novelty do **not** change opportunity ranking. This section does **not** waive existing scoring or mandatory source/identity requirements, owner-only history gates, or contact-ready restrictions; do not treat a research prospect as an approved outreach recipient.
