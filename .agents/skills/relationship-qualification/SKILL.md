# relationship-qualification

Read `../../../references/scoring-rubric.md`. Allocate points out of 30 fit, 25 influence, 20 specific conversation rationale, 15 reachability, 10 location/work mode. Include precise fact URLs, distinguish supported facts from hypotheses. Threshold >=70, but hard gates first: verified current person/title/organization, sourced reason, lawful contact path, no opt-out, private-history novelty check. Don't inflate scores to meet 20. Tucson preferred but high-quality fully remote US accepted, all industries eligible. Return PASS/HOLD/REJECT and numerical breakdown.

## Supervised opportunity pathway assessment — avoid cross-applied requirements

For **DIRECT_HIRING**, the posted position itself is the potential leadership responsibility. Compare the approved candidate profile with the *actual advertised* duties, mandatory experience, credentials, portfolio/people-management scope, and Arizona/Tucson or US-remote feasibility. **Do not require an additional hypothetical leadership role beyond the job**, or mark `CONTRIBUTION_UNCLEAR` merely because the open position belongs to an existing executive's department. Mark unverified ATS acceptance, mandatory fit, geography and named hiring authority as separate research HOLDs; do not invent resume experience, a live vacancy or applicant eligibility.

For **STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION**, no advertised position is proven. Require a source-backed business initiative and a plausible **distinct, bounded internal leadership contribution** that would complement known incumbents rather than duplicate their entire remit. Explain the decision, specific conversation question, possible business measure and remaining hiring uncertainty. A vague "AI strategy" pitch is insufficient. Do not use a publicly accessible executive to rescue an unrelated opportunity.

For either pathway, preserve valuable research-only people/roles even when direct contact verification, private novelty or application status is unresolved. This section does **not** waive existing scoring, mandatory source/identity requirements, owner-only history gates or contact-ready restrictions; do not treat a research prospect as an approved outreach recipient.
