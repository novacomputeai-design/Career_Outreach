## Procedure

Conditionally activate only for a genuine planned/confirmed conversation; do not invent a meeting. Summarize exact organizational goals, source-verified AI initiatives, execution maturity UNKNOWN where appropriate, named executive's DOCUMENTED or hypothesized responsibility, contribution assumptions, conflicts and open questions. Include 30-second truthful employment-oriented positioning using approved resume/Nova experience, three to five informed exploratory questions, and appropriate employment-focused next steps. No Nova services pitch. Private meeting notes outside public Git, no self-authorized invite/calendar/send.

## Output boundary

Return only cited facts, clearly marked hypotheses and reason-coded HOLDs. Record whether this specialist was natively selected, NOT_VERIFIED, or SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE. Research findings and drafts are not contact-ready or sent.
