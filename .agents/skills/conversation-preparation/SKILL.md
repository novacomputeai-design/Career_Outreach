# conversation-preparation

Confirm the actual person, organization and meeting status; no inferred booked meeting. Verify up-to-date priorities with URLs and dates. Draft 30-second professional positioning consistent with the resume, 3–5 thoughtful questions and an appropriate next step. Do not claim hiring interest without evidence. Record useful notes privately; no calendar changes or outreach without explicit authorization.
