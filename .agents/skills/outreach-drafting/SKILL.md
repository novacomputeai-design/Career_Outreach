# outreach-drafting

**V2.1 authority:** Read `../../../references/career-intelligence-operating-contract-v2.1.md` and `../../../config/operating-policy.json` before use. Project `PERSONAL_CAREER_OUTREACH` is personal permanent leadership employment only. This skill cannot certify source truth, run owner-only DPAPI tools, approve sending, auto-apply or change policy. `SEND_AUTHORIZED` remains BLOCKED.

## Procedure

Activate only when requested and prior research/route/relationship review permits proposal. STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION: one accurately cited employer objective, a truthful candidate contribution, respectful exchange ask and hiring status UNCONFIRMED. DIRECT_HIRING: specify actual current requisition, verified mandatory fit and specific interest, without impersonating a recruiter or inventing a hiring sponsor. If strategic executive is merely FUNCTIONALLY_PLAUSIBLE or only an INDIRECT_GENERAL route is known, return SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE or HOLD instead of a targeted draft.

Use natural first-person professional language with specific relevant experience, not robotic claims or Nova Compute sales pitches. Draft is UNSENT and PENDING_CONTENT_APPROVAL only. Gate A exact draft/hash approval does not grant Gate B send permission; SEND_AUTHORIZED remains blocked; no auto-apply, scheduling, inviting or messaging.

## Output boundary

Return only cited facts, clearly marked hypotheses and reason-coded HOLDs. Record whether this specialist was natively selected, NOT_VERIFIED, or SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE. Research findings and drafts are not contact-ready or sent.
