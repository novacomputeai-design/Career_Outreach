## Procedure

Run the native orchestrator for PERSONAL_CAREER_OUTREACH. Begin with candidate evidence, Tucson-first organization discovery and live-source access status. Separate capability_theme (AI_STRATEGY_TRANSFORMATION, ENTERPRISE_PRODUCT_LEADERSHIP, BOTH) from engagement_pathway (STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION, DIRECT_HIRING). Never optimize for 20 new people or a score; 20 remains at most a legacy capacity ceiling.

Required when research is run: organization-discovery, provenance-and-freshness, relationship-qualification, run-quality-audit and daily-executive-brief. Activate leadership-discovery and contact-verification when an executive is investigated; owner-only relationship-memory screening when person-specific contact is contemplated. Activate outreach-drafting only after applicable evidence/route checks and owner request; conversation-preparation and follow-up-planning only for real planned conversations or established relationships; outcome-intelligence and discovery-strategy-optimization when feedback/outcomes exist. Record NATIVE_VERIFIED only upon observed native host activation; otherwise NOT_VERIFIED. Conditional non-invocations are SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE with reason.

Pipeline: organization and AI/product strategy -> specific documented business objective -> initiative and hiring evidence -> executive responsibility graph -> resume-grounded contribution -> independent engagement pathway -> factual audit -> owner opportunity brief. Include research holds and zero useful findings honestly. Contact-ready, send authorization and application submission are always false in the current research process.

## Focused read-only leader-discovery mode

For an owner-requested discovery test, optimize for **a short list of relevant, source-grounded PEOPLE and professional routes**. An employer/initiative is the reason to search, not the final deliverable. A useful row must answer: (1) why this organization now, (2) why this specific current leader, (3) which publicly evidenced professional route can be tried manually, and (4) what remains unknown. The research objective is finding appropriate leaders for the owner's personal career conversations; an open vacancy is not required and must not be invented.

Use organization-discovery + provenance-and-freshness + leadership-discovery + contact-verification + relationship-qualification + run-quality-audit + daily-executive-brief. Keep relationship-memory **owner-only** and all other specialists conditional. For each promising employer, compare the documented initiative owner with a relevant, more accessible operating or product leader before choosing the primary target. Seek an alternate only when its identity and relevance are supportable. Return fewer people rather than filling a quota with CEOs, job titles, profile guesses, or general company inboxes.

**Discovery preflight:** Before searching names, obtain the owner-supplied public-safe list of previously researched organizations/leaders (from the current request or a public-only prior research brief). Do not open private relationship files, guess previous contacts, or claim comprehensive novelty when the list is missing. Label matches ALREADY_RESEARCHED; preserve a genuinely distinct new initiative at the same employer as UPDATED_OPPORTUNITY, not a newly discovered employer or person. Find organization-level triggers FIRST, then select leaders, then seek contacts. For a focused five-leader trial, screen approximately 5–8 distinct organizations if source access allows, then **deeply investigate the best three** before expanding further. The breadth target is a research-effort guide, never a quota. Record each organization's source-backed trigger, investigate the best relevant leaders, and complete a bounded source-backed contact search before stopping. Report the actual organizations and people checked, any source/access limits, and why a promising lead was held instead of silently discarded. Never begin by recycling familiar leaders to fill the list.

**Repeat preflight is mandatory for this mode:** Read `../../../config/public-career-research-baseline.json` before any external discovery. Combine that PUBLIC_RESEARCH_ONLY index with any owner-supplied public research list; check by person+employer and known employer aliases, never by a name alone. Do not classify a person already indexed as NEW_IN_THIS_RESEARCH, even if a different initiative is newly announced. Treat indexed employers as previously screened, while allowing distinct newly evidenced opportunities/people at them. If the file is inaccessible, report REPEAT_STATUS_UNKNOWN; don't claim novelty. The baseline is not relationship history, sending permission or a complete deduplication record. Return proposed *public-only* additions for owner review, but never edit the index during a read-only research run.

**Quality before volume:** For each of the best three employers, explore the decision-owner ladder (business outcome owner → portfolio/product or transformation VP/Director → CIO/CAIO → CEO only when best supported), verify current roles, compare primary versus alternate, and complete a bounded multi-source contact search before moving on. Report a sourced professional contact or an explicit reason-coded hold; never count a known person as newly discovered.

**Focused local diagnostic selection rule:** From the already screened employer pool, deepen only cases with a source-backed Tucson-area initiative (not just a Tucson site); use a separately evidenced Arizona-eligible remote pathway only when the assignment permits remote. For each deep employer, choose a current role-verified **FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET** distinct from its **ACCOUNTABLE_SPONSOR** where appropriate. If the practical VP/Director's current role or direct connection is unknown, record a hold; do not inflate its rank. Run the bounded three-lane contact search, then advance to the next qualified screened employer when no appropriate route is found. Preserve meaningful route-held leads rather than extending the search indefinitely. Require a genuinely differentiated, measurable, candidate-grounded responsibility before presenting a lead as conversation-worthy.

**Final usefulness gate:** Apply the three independently assessed improvements before counting a discovery: (1) shared, named, and purpose-restricted professional routes must not be conflated; (2) consult the complete available public-only cross-run baseline for person and employer repeats; and (3) require a distinct plausible *permanent internal leadership* contribution rather than treating a new factory, AI headline or reachable executive as a career lead. Prefer one well-evidenced conversation-worthy person over a quota. A signed owner post pointing to a shared inbox stays SHARED_INBOX_CONTEXTUAL_INVITATION; an owner/media-only email stays PURPOSE_RESTRICTED_ROUTE_REVIEW. Hold genuinely interesting but overlapping initiatives as PROMISING_RESEARCH_ONLY. No automatic contact/draft/application.

**Small supervised discovery pilot:** Freeze three explicitly named employers at the start of the run and do not backfill. Screen employer leadership-demand evidence first, tag `VERIFIED_LEADERSHIP_OPENING`, `STRATEGIC_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL` or `INITIATIVE_ONLY_NO_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL` with cited source and date, and skip deep executive/contact search when there is only a generic initiative. Prefer unresearched Tucson employers, but retain demonstrably new opportunities at known employers with repeat labels. For qualifying cases only, compare sponsor vs practical leader, verify work mode and meaningful distinct candidate fit, then follow bounded contact search. Report all original employers including honest HOLDs; do not claim this three-employer diagnostic satisfies the separate V2.1 six-employer qualification gate. No external action.

**Decision-maker depth diagnostic (no employer expansion):** When asked to improve who-to-contact accuracy, reuse the **two already shortlisted employers** rather than running discovery again. For each: (i) verify vacancy or initiative status as evidence permits, (ii) map up to three relevant current people with cited individual-to-decision edges, (iii) identify the accountable hiring/strategy sponsor and best practically suitable first-conversation target, (iv) run exact-person, three-lane contact searches for primary plus one genuinely relevant alternate, (v) report named person contact, appropriate official application process, profile-only or route HOLD separately. Never substitute a recruiter for a decision-maker without stated authority, and never treat a general company form as a named route. Report **0 suitable direct person routes** when that is the evidenced outcome. No new organizations, no private owner history and no outreach.

Use the presentation contract in `../../../references/leader-contact-discovery-brief.md`. A public person-specific work route is a research FINDING, not CONTACT_READY or permission to message. A profile without a confirmed way to message remains PROFILE_ONLY; missing identity/route is CONTACT_HOLD with a concrete next research action. No owner-memory access, drafting, sending, application or changes to files unless separately requested.
