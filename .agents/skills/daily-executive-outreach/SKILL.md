# daily-executive-outreach

**V2.1 authority:** Read `../../../references/career-intelligence-operating-contract-v2.1.md` and `../../../config/operating-policy.json` before use. Project `PERSONAL_CAREER_OUTREACH` is personal permanent leadership employment only. This skill cannot certify source truth, run owner-only DPAPI tools, approve sending, auto-apply or change policy. `SEND_AUTHORIZED` remains BLOCKED.

## Scope and activation precedence — apply before mode-specific examples

**The owner's current requested scope controls the run.** Choose one research mode first:
- **FIXED_EMPLOYER / FIXED_REQUISITION:** Research only the named employers or job IDs. Do **not** restart broad 5–8-employer discovery or impose a Tucson-local pilot rule. The earlier two-employer depth checks are examples of this mode, not permanent mandatory sample sizes.
- **BROAD_DISCOVERY:** When no employer set is prescribed, use Tucson/Southern Arizona first and feasible Arizona-eligible US-remote second; screen a small diverse pool and deepen only the best evidence-supported opportunities.
- **EXPLICIT_LOCAL_DIAGNOSTIC / THREE_EMPLOYER_PILOT:** Apply the special local-only or frozen three-company constraints **only if specifically requested**. Do not apply those test rules to an ordinary career-discovery run.

Choose `DIRECT_HIRING` or `STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION` separately for each opportunity. A sourced official senior vacancy (even if application status, location, or the manager still needs rechecking) **triggers leadership-discovery** and candidate-to-advertised-role fit review; do not stop person mapping solely because no manager was named. A credible specific strategic leadership signal retained for person-targeted research **also triggers leadership-discovery**, with hiring intent still unconfirmed. A generic initiative lacking a plausible leadership mandate stays research context; do not chase executive contact details without a meaningful conversation thesis. Preserve all status/feasibility HOLDs as independent unknowns, not evidence that the person research already occurred.

**Handoff is explicit:** leadership-discovery maps owner, relevant operating/product/data leader and exact-role promoter/recruiting entry as supported; it compares the people **before** selecting a first-conversation target plus at most one alternate. Then contact-verification performs its bounded three-lane contact search for those selected people. A recruiter or role promoter is an appropriate hiring-process entry candidate, never automatically the business decision-owner. An official application route stays separate from named-person contact. The run-quality audit verifies this handoff or records why it was skipped. No new employer search, contact approval or sending follows from this rule.

## Procedure

Run the native orchestrator for PERSONAL_CAREER_OUTREACH. Begin with candidate evidence, Tucson-first organization discovery and live-source access status. Separate capability_theme (AI_STRATEGY_TRANSFORMATION, ENTERPRISE_PRODUCT_LEADERSHIP, BOTH) from engagement_pathway (STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION, DIRECT_HIRING). Never optimize for 20 new people or a score; 20 remains at most a legacy capacity ceiling.

Required when research is run: organization-discovery, provenance-and-freshness, relationship-qualification, run-quality-audit and daily-executive-brief. Activate leadership-discovery for each sourced direct-hiring opportunity and each credible strategic opportunity selected for person research, even when no executive is yet named; hand the selected primary and justified alternate to contact-verification after the candidate comparison; owner-only relationship-memory screening when person-specific contact is contemplated. Activate outreach-drafting only after applicable evidence/route checks and owner request; conversation-preparation and follow-up-planning only for real planned conversations or established relationships; outcome-intelligence and discovery-strategy-optimization when feedback/outcomes exist. Record NATIVE_VERIFIED only upon observed native host activation; otherwise NOT_VERIFIED. Conditional non-invocations are SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE with reason.

Pipeline: organization and AI/product strategy -> specific documented business objective -> initiative and hiring evidence -> executive responsibility graph -> resume-grounded contribution -> independent engagement pathway -> factual audit -> owner opportunity brief. Include research holds and zero useful findings honestly. Contact-ready, send authorization and application submission are always false in the current research process.

## Focused read-only leader-discovery mode

For an owner-requested discovery test, optimize for **a short list of relevant, source-grounded PEOPLE and professional routes**. An employer/initiative is the reason to search, not the final deliverable. A useful row must answer: (1) why this organization now, (2) why this specific current leader, (3) which publicly evidenced professional route can be tried manually, and (4) what remains unknown. The research objective is finding appropriate leaders for the owner's personal career conversations; an open vacancy is not required and must not be invented.

Use organization-discovery + provenance-and-freshness + leadership-discovery + contact-verification + relationship-qualification + run-quality-audit + daily-executive-brief. Keep relationship-memory **owner-only** and all other specialists conditional. For each promising employer, compare the documented initiative owner with a relevant, more accessible operating or product leader before choosing the primary target. Seek an alternate only when its identity and relevance are supportable. Return fewer people rather than filling a quota with CEOs, job titles, profile guesses, or general company inboxes.

**Discovery preflight:** Before searching names, obtain the owner-supplied public-safe list of previously researched organizations/leaders (from the current request or a public-only prior research brief). Do not open private relationship files, guess previous contacts, or claim comprehensive novelty when the list is missing. Label matches ALREADY_RESEARCHED; preserve a genuinely distinct new initiative at the same employer as UPDATED_OPPORTUNITY, not a newly discovered employer or person. Find organization-level triggers FIRST, then select leaders, then seek contacts. For a focused five-leader trial, screen approximately 5–8 distinct organizations if source access allows, then **deeply investigate the best three** before expanding further. The breadth target is a research-effort guide, never a quota. Record each organization's source-backed trigger, investigate the best relevant leaders, and complete a bounded source-backed contact search before stopping. Report the actual organizations and people checked, any source/access limits, and why a promising lead was held instead of silently discarded. Never begin by recycling familiar leaders to fill the list.

**Repeat preflight is mandatory for this mode:** Read `../../../config/public-career-research-baseline.json` before any external discovery. Combine that PUBLIC_RESEARCH_ONLY index with any owner-supplied public research list; check by person+employer and known employer aliases, never by a name alone. Do not classify a person already indexed as NEW_IN_THIS_RESEARCH, even if a different initiative is newly announced. Treat indexed employers as previously screened, while allowing distinct newly evidenced opportunities/people at them. If the file is inaccessible, report REPEAT_STATUS_UNKNOWN; don't claim novelty. The baseline is not relationship history, sending permission or a complete deduplication record. Return proposed *public-only* additions for owner review, but never edit the index during a read-only research run.

**Quality before volume:** For each of the best three employers, explore the decision-owner ladder (business outcome owner → portfolio/product or transformation VP/Director → CIO/CAIO → CEO only when best supported), verify current roles, compare primary versus alternate, and complete a bounded multi-source contact search before moving on. Report a sourced professional contact or an explicit reason-coded hold; never count a known person as newly discovered.

**Focused local diagnostic selection rule (ONLY if requested):** From the already screened employer pool, deepen only cases with a source-backed Tucson-area initiative (not just a Tucson site); use a separately evidenced Arizona-eligible remote pathway only when the assignment permits remote. For each deep employer, choose a current role-verified **FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET** distinct from its **ACCOUNTABLE_SPONSOR** where appropriate. If the practical VP/Director's current role or direct connection is unknown, record a hold; do not inflate its rank. Run the bounded three-lane contact search, then advance to the next qualified screened employer when no appropriate route is found. Preserve meaningful route-held leads rather than extending the search indefinitely. Require a genuinely differentiated, measurable, candidate-grounded responsibility before presenting a lead as conversation-worthy.

**Final usefulness gate:** Apply the three independently assessed improvements before counting a discovery: (1) shared, named, and purpose-restricted professional routes must not be conflated; (2) consult the complete available public-only cross-run baseline for person and employer repeats; and (3) require a distinct plausible *permanent internal leadership* contribution for STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION; for DIRECT_HIRING evaluate fit against the advertised position itself rather than requiring a second invented role. Prefer one well-evidenced conversation-worthy person over a quota. A signed owner post pointing to a shared inbox stays SHARED_INBOX_CONTEXTUAL_INVITATION; an owner/media-only email stays PURPOSE_RESTRICTED_ROUTE_REVIEW. Hold genuinely interesting but overlapping initiatives as PROMISING_RESEARCH_ONLY. No automatic contact/draft/application.

**Small supervised discovery pilot (ONLY if requested):** Freeze three explicitly named employers at the start of the run and do not backfill. Screen employer leadership-demand evidence first, tag `VERIFIED_LEADERSHIP_OPENING`, `STRATEGIC_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL` or `INITIATIVE_ONLY_NO_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL` with cited source and date, and skip deep executive/contact search when there is only a generic initiative. Prefer unresearched Tucson employers, but retain demonstrably new opportunities at known employers with repeat labels. For qualifying cases only, compare sponsor vs practical leader, verify work mode and meaningful distinct candidate fit, then follow bounded contact search. Report all original employers including honest HOLDs; do not claim this three-employer diagnostic satisfies the separate V2.1 six-employer qualification gate. No external action.

**Fixed-employer research procedure:** For explicitly named employers or requisitions, verify each vacancy/initiative and status as evidence permits, then map the accountable decision owner, operating/product leader and publicly identifiable exact-role promoters or recruiters. Do not let an organizational chart or an arbitrary three-person search cap preempt the exact-role post search. Compare supported candidates; choose the best FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET plus at most one ALTERNATE, with sponsor versus recruiting-process roles distinct. Verify an appropriate professional route or report PROFILE_ONLY, role-specific invitation/promoter evidence, official application and CONTACT_HOLD independently. **Do not backfill other employers** or treat "apply today" as a verified personal inbox.

Use the presentation contract in `../../../references/leader-contact-discovery-brief.md`. A public person-specific work route is a research FINDING, not CONTACT_READY or permission to message. A profile without a confirmed way to message remains PROFILE_ONLY; missing identity/route is CONTACT_HOLD with a concrete next research action. No owner-memory access, drafting, sending, application or changes to files unless separately requested.
