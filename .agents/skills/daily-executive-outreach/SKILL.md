## Supervised decision-maker discovery — mode and handoff

**Mode precedence:** Follow the owner's explicit scope first. For FIXED_EMPLOYER/FIXED_REQUISITION research, investigate only the named employers and roles; do not restart a Tucson employer scan or apply older sample-size and daily-quota targets. For BROAD_DISCOVERY, research Tucson/Southern Arizona first and feasible US-remote second. Apply a special local or frozen-employer pilot constraint only if the owner explicitly requests that pilot. These modes govern source research, not contact-ready certification or authorization.

**Opportunity-first order:** organization-discovery creates and compares opportunity records using `opportunity-assessment.md`. Rank these before person research. A DIRECT_HIRING opportunity remains distinct from its people and routes; a STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION opportunity has no proven vacancy and needs a bounded, non-duplicative contribution. Work-mode, qualification, ATS acceptance, contact, and novelty uncertainties remain separate fields. Missing personal contact information never lowers opportunity quality.

**Activation trigger:** After opportunity ranking, start leadership-discovery for selected plausible DIRECT_HIRING roles **even when the hiring manager is unnamed**, and for specific STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION initiatives with a credible bounded contribution. Hand over the exact opportunity record. A live ATS or location HOLD remains separate; do not call an opening active on indexed text alone. A generic AI announcement without a meaningful leadership connection does not justify a broad contact hunt.

**Ordered handoff:** For each selected opportunity, research the official role/reporting line and closest business/product/data/AI operating function; search exact job title/requisition and employer posts for a named process contact or promoter. Compare materially relevant people before recommending one FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET and, if supported, one alternate. Contact-verification checks those exact names with bounded public, purpose-appropriate sources. Keep ACCOUNTABLE_SPONSOR, functional leader, recruiting-process contact, public role promotion/invitation, person-specific route, and official application route distinct. A recruiter can be the best process entry without becoming a proven hiring decision-maker. These person findings never alter opportunity rank.

**Research-only output:** Show documented opportunity, strongest current people, source-based reason to connect, route class and unresolved checks. Preserve promising PROFILE_ONLY or CONTACT_HOLD findings without declaring CONTACT_READY, personal novelty, a currently accepting vacancy, or send/apply authority. The operator-only encrypted relationship screen and all existing review and send blocks remain unchanged; no protected memory is opened in Codex.
