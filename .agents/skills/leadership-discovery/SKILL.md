# leadership-discovery

For each organization research official people, leadership, governance, speaker, industry group and business association sources. Record candidate full name, current claimed title, organization, official bio/source URL/date and why their influence is plausible. Classify as potential hiring decision-maker, hiring influencer, or connector; indicate UNVERIFIED hiring power unless documented. Consider functional leaders beyond product and IT when interests overlap. Avoid guesses, fabricated contacts and restricted platform scraping. Send people, not company names, to contact-verification.

## Supervised selection procedure — discover before narrowing

Begin after a provisional opportunity record and ranking exist. Preserve the opportunity type, role/initiative evidence, candidate-fit and feasibility findings, and unresolved questions through people research. Leadership research can reveal material facts about organizational needs, timing, incumbents, responsibilities, decision rights, role scope, or actual candidate feasibility; when sourced, feed those facts back to opportunity assessment and revise its rank if warranted. Record the old rank, new evidence, source/date, rationale, and new rank. Contact accessibility, route availability, recruiter responsiveness, or relationship-history/novelty results cannot change opportunity rank.

**Trigger:** Start for any sourced DIRECT_HIRING opportunity selected for review, including one with an unnamed manager, and for credible STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION opportunities chosen for person-targeted research. Do not assume current vacancy acceptance or hiring from an unverified source.

1. **Discover:** Make one bounded source-led pass through employer role/reporting and leadership pages, the closest product/data/AI/operating business function, and an **exact title or requisition + employer** public recruiter/promoter search. Investigate current employee hiring posts as well as org charts; check one promising newly found source for corroboration if necessary. Do not stop after the first CTO/CEO or cap discovery at three names. A three-person limit is NOT a candidate search limit. Record failed searches and access gaps; do not chase unrelated executives indefinitely.
2. **Verify and classify:** Independently source the current name, employer, title, date, role-to-opportunity link, and possible departures/namesakes. Distinguish ACCOUNTABLE_SPONSOR, current operating/product/data leader, HIRING_SPONSOR if directly evidenced, RECRUITING_PROCESS_CONTACT, ROLE_SPECIFIC_PROMOTER and FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET. An employer listing that says "reports to CTO" supports a reporting line, **not** an assertion that the named CTO personally manages recruiting. A recruiter or public promoter may be a hiring-process entry without confirmed interview/budget authority.
3. **Compare after searching:** Consider all materially relevant people surfaced by the bounded source passes (including a fourth person who promoted the exact role). Rank for the actual hiring or strategy conversation by documented decision connection, current role, and specific contribution relevance before seniority. Accessibility may help select the person to approach but is not organizational evidence and never feeds back into opportunity rank. Explain why an accessible recruiter is or is not a better first *process* contact than a functional executive. For DIRECT_HIRING, match the advertised role; for STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION, test a distinct bounded internal contribution.
4. **Reassess the opportunity:** Return to opportunity assessment when leadership evidence changes understanding of the organization's need, timing, incumbents, responsibilities/authority, scope, or candidate feasibility. Keep the original assessment and record a concise evidence-linked revision. Do not revise for a newly discovered email, public profile, response likelihood, or relationship novelty.
5. **Handoff:** Recommend one PRIMARY FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET and at most one justified ALTERNATE, separately naming the ACCOUNTABLE_SPONSOR when different. Pass each selected person's exact name, current-role evidence, role/requisition connection, promoter post and source date, authority limits and purpose to contact-verification. The **two-person limit applies to final recommendations only**, not candidate discovery.
6. **Report honestly:** If identity or sponsorship is unsupported, mark it UNKNOWN or HOLD; do not invent the hiring manager. Preserve a promising person as a research prospect when no legitimate person-specific work route is found. A professional profile or vacancy promotion is not a verified inbox, invitation, or approval to contact.
