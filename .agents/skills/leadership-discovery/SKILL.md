# leadership-discovery

**V2.1 authority:** Read `../../../references/career-intelligence-operating-contract-v2.1.md` and `../../../config/operating-policy.json` before use. Project `PERSONAL_CAREER_OUTREACH` is personal permanent leadership employment only. This skill cannot certify source truth, run owner-only DPAPI tools, approve sending, auto-apply or change policy. `SEND_AUTHORIZED` remains BLOCKED.

## Procedure

Identify six distinct relationship functions as relevant: STRATEGIC_SPONSOR, BUSINESS_OUTCOME_OWNER, AI_OR_TECHNICAL_EXECUTION_OWNER, PRODUCT_PORTFOLIO_OWNER, HIRING_SPONSOR and INTRODUCER. A person may hold several roles, but each person-to-initiative edge needs its own evidence. DOCUMENTED requires a visited source explicitly connecting that person to the initiative or hiring decision. A current CIO/CTO/CEO title without that connection is FUNCTIONALLY_PLAUSIBLE or UNKNOWN, never established sponsorship.

Verify person identity, organization affiliation and up-to-date role separately. Before promoting CURRENT affiliation, search for departures, recent role changes, and independent contradictory sources. A recently retrieved old/undated official biography is not sufficient when a newer credible source describes the person as former; record both sources and mark SOURCE_CONFLICT/CURRENT_EXECUTIVE_AFFILIATION_CONFLICT until reconciled. Distinguish an industry advisor or introducer who previously worked at the employer from a current internal decision-maker. Do not silently erase the former employee as a potential relationship, but do not assign them the employer initiative. No guessed hiring power or contact route. Strong strategic research or an official hiring vacancy may be preserved with an unknown executive and marked EXECUTIVE_OWNER_UNVERIFIED; do not invent a name to fill the packet. Output sourced responsibility graph and prioritized research questions.

## Select the right person for a conversation — one ordered procedure

**Trigger.** Run for every sourced `DIRECT_HIRING` opportunity and every `STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION` opportunity explicitly retained for person-targeted research. A vacancy does not need to name a hiring manager before person research starts. A current-status or Arizona/work-mode HOLD does not prove the role is closed and does not, by itself, prohibit bounded ownership research; the hold still blocks claims of a current actionable position. For an initiative-only announcement with no plausible leadership contribution, stop before a broad contact hunt and record the missing business evidence.

1. **Discover candidates from sources, not titles alone.** Perform one bounded pass across (a) employer job/requisition/reporting and official leadership or business-unit pages; (b) the operating/product/data/transformation group closest to the decision; and (c) **Exact-opportunity promoter discovery:** search the **exact job title or requisition ID** and employer (or documented initiative title) with hiring/recruiting terms, current public employer recruiting posts and professional posts by employees. Follow one promising source lead for corroboration when justified. This is bounded source coverage, **not a hard cap of three discovered people**. A CEO, CIO or CTO biography is not an endpoint; consider an operating VP/Director and any relevant recruiter or employee who promoted the **exact** opening. Record attempted searches, negative findings, and access limits. Do not chase unrelated names or search indefinitely.

2. **Map roles and verify identities.** For each materially relevant person found, verify **current identity and affiliation** independently of initiative responsibility; check departures, namesakes, contradictory sources and historic VP titles. Classify STRATEGIC_SPONSOR, BUSINESS_OUTCOME_OWNER, AI_OR_TECHNICAL_EXECUTION_OWNER, PRODUCT_PORTFOLIO_OWNER, HIRING_SPONSOR, INTRODUCER, RECRUITING_PROCESS_CONTACT or `ROLE_SPECIFIC_PROMOTER` as supported. The **ACCOUNTABLE_SPONSOR** is the most evidenced decision owner; the recruiting-process entry or publicly available role promoter may be a separate person. A post must have a source URL, date, exact opportunity match, and characterization as a first-person invitation versus repost. `DOCUMENTED_OWNER` / `DOCUMENTED` requires a visited source explicitly linking that person to the exact initiative/decision; a title or business function alone is `FUNCTIONALLY_PLAUSIBLE` or UNKNOWN. A reported `reports to CTO` hierarchy establishes the role-level reporting line, **not proof a named CTO is the hiring decision-maker**. Promotion of a vacancy does not establish hiring authority or message availability.

3. **Decision-maker selection — compare after discovery.** Consider **all materially relevant candidates identified in the bounded passes**, even when that means four or more names, before selecting a **FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET** and **at most one ALTERNATE** for the final brief. The **two-person limit applies to final recommendations only**. Compare (a) exact person-to-opportunity responsibility evidence, (b) influence over the specific business outcome or advertised role, (c) professional-conversation relevance and the owner's evidenced experience, (d) legitimate professional accessibility, and only then (e) seniority. Do not let a published address rescue `LOW_CAREER_FIT` or elevate an irrelevant recruiter above a decision owner. For direct hiring, a purpose-aligned recruiting contact may be a practical first *hiring-process* entry while a functional hiring sponsor remains distinct and unknown. For strategic exploration, prefer the outcome owner or operating product/AI leader closest to the real initiative over CEO prestige, absent contrary evidence. Explain why primary beats alternate and which role each can legitimately play.

4. **Handoff to contact-verification.** Supply the chosen primary and supported alternate with exact person name, current employer/title evidence and date, source-backed initiative/requisition link, classified decision function, documented-versus-plausible authority, promotion/invitation source when any, and why the person was selected. Require separate route checks for each where applicable; official ATS applications, role-specific invitations, profiles and named professional work contacts remain separate classifications. If the practical leader lacks current identity/scope evidence or a route, report `FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET_CANDIDATE — RESEARCH_HOLD` rather than upgrading to contact-ready. Explicitly preserve `ACCOUNTABLE_SPONSOR` separately from `FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET` when they differ.

5. **Stop and report honestly.** If no reliable person-to-opportunity edge survives, use `EXECUTIVE_OWNER_UNVERIFIED` and list the highest-value unresolved check. If contact search finds no suitable route, preserve the promising opportunity with its source-backed person map and contact HOLD; never fabricate a manager or a work email. A professional profile or promotion is evidence of identity or possible reason to connect, not authorization, confirmed deliverability or an invented invitation. No file, contact, application or approval state is changed by this specialist.

## Output boundary

Return only cited facts, clearly marked hypotheses and reason-coded HOLDs. Record whether this specialist was natively selected, NOT_VERIFIED, or SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE. Research findings and drafts are not contact-ready or sent.
