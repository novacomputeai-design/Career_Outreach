## Procedure

Discover any-industry employers Tucson/Southern Arizona first, followed by genuinely feasible US-remote employers. Do not privilege high-profile names, AI keyword frequency or job aggregators over actual evidence.

For every candidate record employer and work mode; what leadership publicly states; business outcome; AI/product/operational initiative; tangible pilot/deployment/partnership/organizational activity when evidenced; maturity as SOURCE_SUPPORTED or UNKNOWN; relevant current vacancy when verified; conflicting/unknown details and exact research questions. Document exact visited source URL, publisher, original lineage, passage, retrieved_at and published_on or UNDATED. A company saying it uses AI does not prove it needs an AI strategy, new executive or funded position. No public information means UNKNOWN, not absent AI.

In focused leader-discovery mode, first build a **small organization-first candidate pool** from different sectors and employer sources. For a focused request for up to five leaders, screen approximately 5–8 distinct employers with source-supported triggers when public coverage permits, then prioritize the best three for deeper executive-and-contact investigation. Research fewer credible employers in depth before broadening the search; do not equate a long list of shallow searches with useful discovery. Start with employer announcements, official AI/product transformation pages, and primary job postings; search executive names only AFTER sourcing a specific employer trigger. If time/access yields fewer organizations, disclose the actual count and why; never fabricate breadth or fill a quota with weak employers. Do not mistake a search query or snippet for a visited employer source.

Apply an owner-provided **public-only previously-researched exclusion list** to tag repeats. Keep previously researched leaders or employers as ALREADY_RESEARCHED, not NEW_DISCOVERY. A new independently sourced initiative/requisition at a known employer can be UPDATED_OPPORTUNITY but is not a new organization/person; preserve it separately if valuable. No list provided means REPEAT_STATUS_UNKNOWN, not presumed new. Do not consult DPAPI, private relationship history, or user-specific contact notes.

Before naming leaders, load the public-only previously researched baseline at `../../../config/public-career-research-baseline.json` and any public-only owner-provided updates. Track employer and person repeats **separately**. Known employers may yield a new initiative or different current leader; a previous employer or person's presence in a fresh search does not reset their research history. Do not enter a public career-research name or employer in the private relationship-memory gate, and do not overwrite or append the baseline in a read-only run. Missing/incomplete baseline means REPEAT_STATUS_UNKNOWN, not proven novelty.

## Tucson-local trigger decision gate

Treat the Tucson-first instruction as a **geographically evidenced opportunity priority**, not merely an employer-address preference. For each candidate, report three separately sourced facts: (1) employer has a Tucson/Southern Arizona operation, (2) the specific business change affects that operation or has a named regional owner, and (3) the possible internal leadership position could realistically be based there. `LOCAL_SITE_CONFIRMED` alone is **not** `LOCAL_INITIATIVE_CONFIRMED`. If a source describes a Tempe/Phoenix or other non-Tucson expansion while another source only identifies a Tucson office, mark `LOCAL_INITIATIVE_UNVERIFIED`; keep the employer in a location/feasibility HOLD rather than promote it as a Tucson deep-investigation win. A separate independently verified Arizona-eligible US-remote opportunity can still qualify on the REMOTE track. Do not assume local leadership hiring or site ownership.

In a bounded local diagnostic, prioritize deeply investigating employers with *both* current initiative evidence and a demonstrated Southern Arizona connection. If fewer than three survive, show the shortfall; do not backfill with a statewide initiative merely to fill three slots. Track `TUCSON_INITIATIVE_SUPPORTED`, `REGIONAL_EMPLOYER_ONLY`, and `REMOTE_US_FEASIBILITY_UNVERIFIED` as **human-facing research annotations**, not new schema fields. 

Output organization + separate initiative records and candidate business objectives, not invented executive contact lists. Neither a Tucson location nor an interesting announcement alone establishes meaningful candidate contribution.

## Research-history coverage and opportunity-first selection

Read `../../../config/public-career-research-baseline.json` **before** discovery and compare all newly found employers and named people. The index includes screened employers and researched leaders from earlier read-only tests; it is a *partial public research index*, not protected relationship history. For each selected record keep **employer_seen_before**, **person_seen_before**, and **new_distinct_initiative** conceptually separate. A previously screened employer may contain a genuinely new named owner or business initiative, but never label that employer as first discovered; a person already researched is never new-to-public-research because a newer article names them. Propose public-only index additions for owner review after the run, never silently edit the file during read-only research. Do not infer private relationship novelty from absence in the index.

Prefer a source-backed business **decision with unresolved adoption, product, implementation or cross-functional coordination work** over a generic facility opening, executive biography or fashionable AI announcement. A new factory hiring production staff is not evidence of a senior AI/product leadership opening; retain factual change but classify its personal-career fit separately. Direct job opportunities require a live, official employer ATS check and Arizona/Tucson or US-remote feasibility evidence.

## Opportunity-demand signal before leader/contact expansion

For focused personal-career discovery, **screen for evidence of a leadership-relevant opportunity before looking for a leader's email**. For each employer, distinguish and cite one of three *report-only* evidence levels:

- `VERIFIED_LEADERSHIP_OPENING`: a current, read official employer/ATS senior product, AI strategy, innovation or enterprise transformation role, with Tucson/Arizona or verified US-remote feasibility and mandatory requirements still to be checked.
- `STRATEGIC_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL`: a specific current initiative **plus** an observable shift in responsibility, new operating capability, organizational appointment/mandate, material scaling decision, or transformation work needing a cross-functional owner. This is an evidence-supported **reason to investigate**, never an invented hiring gap or vacant position.
- `INITIATIVE_ONLY_NO_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL`: a project, new building, technology announcement or operational expansion with no observed internal leadership mandate or plausible distinct contribution. Preserve as context/hold; do not spend deep contact-search effort merely because executives are named.

For the first small read-only pilot, precommit **three named Tucson-area employers** before outcome research and keep all three in the denominator. Prefer employers absent from the partial public baseline; allow a previously screened employer only for a separately sourced new business initiative or current official requisition and label employer repeat. Research each employer's leadership-demand evidence **before** selecting a named person. If evidence is insufficient, report HOLD rather than backfilling with an attractive CEO, generic AI initiative or easier-to-contact business. Report sources, evidence age, local initiative relevance, current work feasibility and one decisive next check. This is a focused diagnostic protocol, **not** the six-employer V2.1 field-qualification or production gate.

## Output boundary

Return only cited facts, clearly marked hypotheses and reason-coded HOLDs. Record whether this specialist was natively selected, NOT_VERIFIED, or SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE. Research findings and drafts are not contact-ready or sent.
