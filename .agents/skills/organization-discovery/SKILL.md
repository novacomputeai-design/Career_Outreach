# organization-discovery

Read public candidate profile and source policy. Rotate through Tech Parks Arizona and tenants, local employers, public/university/healthcare organizations, business associations, corporate sites, regional news and relevant remote-friendly national employers. Do not select by industry label alone. For each organization record name, location/work mode, observed recent strategic initiative or leadership context, source/date, connection to the candidate's transferable capabilities and confidence. Distinguish a plausible need from a documented vacancy. Return a diverse, ranked pool with SOURCE_OBSERVED versus HYPOTHESIS labels. Never invent an organization, role or event; don't force volume.

## Opportunity trigger for supervised people research

Report DIRECT_HIRING when a specific employer or official ATS senior AI/product/transformation opening is evidenced; distinguish a confirmed currently accepting vacancy from a recent indexed posting whose status is unknown. A missing named hiring manager does not prevent leadership-discovery of credible role owners and publicly named promoters. Report STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION only when an observed business initiative supports a plausible, bounded leadership conversation; do not infer a vacancy from AI headlines. Generic initiatives without distinct career relevance remain research context and do not trigger a deep contact hunt. Fixed named employer/requisition scope from the owner overrides broad organization discovery. Work-mode, required-qualification and ATS acceptance uncertainty are separate research holds, not evidence that a person search happened.
