# organization-discovery

Read the public candidate profile, source policy, and `../../../references/opportunity-assessment.md`. Research both advertised senior product, AI, innovation, and transformation roles and organizations with substantive initiatives that could support a bounded strategic leadership conversation. Search Tucson/Southern Arizona first, then feasible U.S.-remote opportunities; do not select by industry label alone. For each lead, capture the opportunity record and provisional ranking before researching people. For direct roles, compare actual duties and mandatory qualifications to candidate evidence; established unmet or unverified mandatory requirements affect candidate feasibility and ranking, while the role's organizational opportunity quality remains separately reportable. Compare related openings and initiatives within the same organization and explain why the strongest one leads. Record sources, dates, status evidence, location and actual eligibility, candidate evidence/gaps, organizational need, leadership scope, career potential, and confidence. Keep `DIRECT_HIRING` distinct from `STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION`; an initiative is not a vacancy. Never invent a role, need, qualification, or eligibility, and do not force volume.

## Opportunity trigger for supervised people research

Report DIRECT_HIRING when a specific employer or official ATS senior AI/product/transformation opening is evidenced; distinguish a confirmed currently accepting vacancy from a recent indexed posting whose status is unknown. Report STRATEGIC_EXPLORATION only when an observed business initiative supports a plausible, bounded leadership contribution; do not infer a vacancy from AI headlines. Generic initiatives without distinct career relevance remain research context. Rank and compare opportunities provisionally before triggering person research. A missing named hiring manager or personal work email does not lower opportunity quality or candidate feasibility. Leadership research may revise ranking on sourced facts about need, timing, incumbents, responsibilities, authority, scope, or candidate feasibility; record the before/after rationale. Contact accessibility and relationship novelty never alter rankings. Fixed named employer/requisition scope from the owner overrides broad discovery. Work-mode, actual eligibility, mandatory-qualification, and ATS-status findings remain distinct.
