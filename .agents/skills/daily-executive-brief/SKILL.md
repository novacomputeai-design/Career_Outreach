# daily-executive-brief

**V2.1 authority:** Read `../../../references/career-intelligence-operating-contract-v2.1.md` and `../../../config/operating-policy.json` before use. Project `PERSONAL_CAREER_OUTREACH` is personal permanent leadership employment only. This skill cannot certify source truth, run owner-only DPAPI tools, approve sending, auto-apply or change policy. `SEND_AUTHORIZED` remains BLOCKED.

## Procedure

For each opportunity show employer/location and work eligibility; candidate capability theme and independent engagement pathway; cited business objectives or verified current requisition; explicit strategy/execution/hiring distinctions; role responsibility map (DOCUMENTED/FUNCTIONALLY_PLAUSIBLE/UNKNOWN); approved candidate basis and contribution hypothesis; specific route; research_state, engagement_readiness, owner_decision (if separately operator-provided), missing questions and next action.

Separate manual official application possibility from actual application; no unnamed-manager fabrication. Counts: organizations investigated, distinct initiatives/opportunities, assessable, evidence holds, owner Pursue/Research more/Reject, actual observed engagement and hiring events; people/legacy 20 capacity as secondary only. Keep existing strict 19-column person-only Drive export unchanged. V2.1 opportunity reviews must use separately versioned, safe records; no private feedback to PUBLIC Git or automatic Drive mutation. No sending.

## Versioned review packet
Use `outreach_agent/career_intelligence_packet.py` through the safe offline CLI for V2.1 public-only organization reports. The existing strict 19-column person exporter remains legacy/unchanged; do not insert V2.1 schema keys into it or transfer private feedback to Google Drive. Do not call a structural preflight CONTACT_READY.

## Leader/contact shortlist — mandatory in discovery mode

Use `../../../references/leader-contact-discovery-brief.md` as the default human-facing format in read-only discovery mode. Display PRIMARY leader and optional ALTERNATE separately, plus organization, exact business trigger, candidate-specific reason, CURRENT title and role evidence, initiative-ownership evidence level, professional contact value/type (if public), contact-source URL/date, and recommended manual research step.

Split outcomes into **PERSON_ROUTE_SUPPORTED**, **PROFILE_ONLY**, and **CONTACT_HOLD**; a documented employer opportunity with no safely identified executive may be retained in a separate research backlog, not counted as a located contact. Quote or link sources for name/title, mandate, and contact **independently**. An INDIVIDUAL_PROFILE_IDENTIFIED is not a verified direct message route. Never label a record CONTACT_READY, sent, applied or approved because a contact detail exists; those are separate restricted statuses.

For each result show public-only repeat status (NEW_IN_THIS_RESEARCH / ALREADY_RESEARCHED / UPDATED_OPPORTUNITY / REPEAT_STATUS_UNKNOWN), independently from private relationship novelty (always UNVERIFIED without owner screening). In the headline do not count a prior known leader as newly found; include re-discoveries in a separate updates section. Include a two-sentence specific career-conversation thesis and name existing leadership overlap/unknown where relevant. Show the organizations screened (approximately 5–8 when feasible), the best three investigated in depth, source types checked for each leader's professional route, and why any promising person remains on HOLD. Preserve the separate **PROMISING_RESEARCH_PROSPECTS** section for verified-current-role leaders with a credible conversation thesis but no confirmed direct route or local/remote job feasibility. This is not a count of contact-ready leaders, a job-opening claim, or authorization for outreach. Treat ability to hold an exploratory professional conversation separately from later employment location feasibility; clearly flag the latter as UNKNOWN rather than deleting a useful networking prospect. No count of investigated organizations is a mandate to output unqualified people.

End with short counts: organizations screened, leaders with sourced current roles, documented vs plausible initiative owners, person-specific publicly listed work routes, profile-only contacts, and unresolved contact holds. If zero qualify, say zero with reasons.

## User-facing five-question checkpoint

For each primary leader explicitly answer **who**, **why that leader rather than a CEO/alternate**, **why a specific career conversation makes sense**, **what published professional route exists and its age/purpose**, and **whether already researched**. Display clear source links for employer trigger, current role, responsibility and contact, with a short per-person contact-search trail. Preserve historical/purpose-restricted contacts as research evidence, not as currently suitable outreach paths. Include separate counts for (a) PERSON_ROUTE_SUPPORTED with current appropriate sources, (b) PROFILE_ONLY, (c) HISTORICAL_PERSON_WORK_CONTACT_RECHECK or PURPOSE_RESTRICTED_ROUTE_REVIEW and (d) CONTACT_HOLD, while retaining the existing protected CONTACT_READY=false boundary.

The final report should note which known people were excluded or updated using `config/public-career-research-baseline.json`. When the baseline was unavailable, state repeat status UNKNOWN; never invent a "new person" count from absence in an untrusted source.

## Focused diagnostic result accounting

Report `LOCAL_INITIATIVE_CONFIRMED` versus `LOCAL_INITIATIVE_UNVERIFIED` explicitly and never treat an unrelated Arizona project as a Tucson opportunity. For each deep employer list: ACCOUNTABLE_SPONSOR; FIRST_CONVERSATION_TARGET (or candidate on hold); reason for that distinction; evidence grade for each person's initiative connection; contact-search lanes actually completed; and one bounded measurable contribution with known overlap. If the best target is still a hypothetical or unreachable executive, show it as **PROMISING_RESEARCH_PROSPECTS**, not a newly contactable lead. After CONTACT_SEARCH_COMPLETED_NO_SUITABLE_ROUTE, show the next screened employer examined or state no source-supported substitute exists. Keep the local-first usefulness assessment distinct from novelty and CONTACT_READY=false.

## Three-item human acceptance gate

Before displaying a recommendation, answer in plain language: **(1) Why is this a worthwhile personal career conversation rather than only a newsworthy project? (2) Was the employer or person already researched? (3) Is the discovered route actually addressed to the person and appropriate for the intended conversation?** Keep `CONVERSATION_WORTH_EXPLORING`, `PROMISING_RESEARCH_ONLY` and `LOW_CAREER_FIT` separate from `PERSON_ROUTE_SUPPORTED`, `SHARED_INBOX_CONTEXTUAL_INVITATION`, `PURPOSE_RESTRICTED_ROUTE_REVIEW`, `PROFILE_ONLY` and `CONTACT_HOLD`. Shared address plus an owner signature is **not** `PERSON_ROUTE_SUPPORTED`.

Report newly discovered people/employers against `config/public-career-research-baseline.json`, and explicitly call out prior-screened matches even if their initiatives are new. Do not count a new email discovery as a new leader. Prefer **one leader genuinely worth discussing** to a long list of available inboxes. All findings remain owner-review research, not CONTACT_READY, send approved or job applications.

## Small-pilot reporting contract

For a **three-employer precommitted pilot**, display the original three employers whether qualified or not, each source-backed trigger, publication date, research-history repeat status and `VERIFIED_LEADERSHIP_OPENING` / `STRATEGIC_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL` / `INITIATIVE_ONLY_NO_LEADERSHIP_SIGNAL`. For any qualifying deeper prospect report the specific internal senior leadership responsibility that may be distinct from the current incumbent, direct-hiring versus exploratory pathway, local/Arizona-remote feasibility, current appropriate person-specific contact or honest route hold, and exact next research action. Separate a genuinely verified current official job from a possible strategic conversation, and separate both from a general interesting initiative. Report **zero qualified conversations** rather than treating all three employer screenings or published inboxes as useful discoveries. Note that this diagnostic is not the production field qualification gate.

## Two-employer decision-maker depth review

When tasked with deepening **who to contact**, keep the two prior employers fixed and report: verified role/initiative mandate; accountable sponsor; operational/functional primary target; any recruiter publicly promoting the exact requisition; how each decision connection was established; primary versus alternate rationale; exact source-bound appropriate contact information, role-specific public invitation, profile-only or no-route hold; and official application separately. Do not count an executive name as success without a defensible reason that *this person* can discuss the opportunity. A role-specific invitation supports a purpose-aligned outreach **hypothesis** only; platform message reachability, consent and current job acceptance still require confirmation. Finish with an evidence-based decision-maker discovery score distinct from person-route accessibility, and no engagement authorization.

## Output boundary

Return only cited facts, clearly marked hypotheses and reason-coded HOLDs. Record whether this specialist was natively selected, NOT_VERIFIED, or SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE. Research findings and drafts are not contact-ready or sent.
