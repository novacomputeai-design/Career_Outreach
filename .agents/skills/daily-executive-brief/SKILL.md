## Procedure

For each opportunity show employer/location and work eligibility; candidate capability theme and independent engagement pathway; cited business objectives or verified current requisition; explicit strategy/execution/hiring distinctions; role responsibility map (DOCUMENTED/FUNCTIONALLY_PLAUSIBLE/UNKNOWN); approved candidate basis and contribution hypothesis; specific route; research_state, engagement_readiness, owner_decision (if separately operator-provided), missing questions and next action.

Separate manual official application possibility from actual application; no unnamed-manager fabrication. Counts: organizations investigated, distinct initiatives/opportunities, assessable, evidence holds, owner Pursue/Research more/Reject, actual observed engagement and hiring events; people/legacy 20 capacity as secondary only. Keep existing strict 19-column person-only Drive export unchanged. V2.1 opportunity reviews must use separately versioned, safe records; no private feedback to PUBLIC Git or automatic Drive mutation. No sending.

## Leader/contact shortlist — mandatory in discovery mode

Use `../../../references/leader-contact-discovery-brief.md` as the default human-facing format in read-only discovery mode. Display PRIMARY leader and optional ALTERNATE separately, plus organization, exact business trigger, candidate-specific reason, CURRENT title and role evidence, initiative-ownership evidence level, professional contact value/type (if public), contact-source URL/date, and recommended manual research step.

Split outcomes into **PERSON_ROUTE_SUPPORTED**, **PROFILE_ONLY**, and **CONTACT_HOLD**; a documented employer opportunity with no safely identified executive may be retained in a separate research backlog, not counted as a located contact. Quote or link sources for name/title, mandate, and contact **independently**. An INDIVIDUAL_PROFILE_IDENTIFIED is not a verified direct message route. Never label a record CONTACT_READY, sent, applied or approved because a contact detail exists; those are separate restricted statuses.

For each result show public-only repeat status (NEW_IN_THIS_RESEARCH / ALREADY_RESEARCHED / UPDATED_OPPORTUNITY / REPEAT_STATUS_UNKNOWN), independently from private relationship novelty (always UNVERIFIED without owner screening). In the headline do not count a prior known leader as newly found; include re-discoveries in a separate updates section. Include a two-sentence specific career-conversation thesis and name existing leadership overlap/unknown where relevant. Show the organizations screened (approximately 5–8 when feasible), the best three investigated in depth, source types checked for each leader's professional route, and why any promising person remains on HOLD. Preserve the separate **PROMISING_RESEARCH_PROSPECTS** section for verified-current-role leaders with a credible conversation thesis but no confirmed direct route or local/remote job feasibility. This is not a count of contact-ready leaders, a job-opening claim, or authorization for outreach. Treat ability to hold an exploratory professional conversation separately from later employment location feasibility; clearly flag the latter as UNKNOWN rather than deleting a useful networking prospect. No count of investigated organizations is a mandate to output unqualified people.

End with short counts: organizations screened, leaders with sourced current roles, documented vs plausible initiative owners, person-specific publicly listed work routes, profile-only contacts, and unresolved contact holds. If zero qualify, say zero with reasons.

## Output boundary

Return only cited facts, clearly marked hypotheses and reason-coded HOLDs. Record whether this specialist was natively selected, NOT_VERIFIED, or SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE. Research findings and drafts are not contact-ready or sent.
