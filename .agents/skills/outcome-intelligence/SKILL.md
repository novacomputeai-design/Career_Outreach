## Procedure

Record owner Pursue/Research more/Reject as human preference with reason and date; never promote source truth or contact permissions. Record only actual owner-reported or independently observed introduction, contacted, replied, met, application, interview, offer and hired events, with actor, date, provenance and opportunity linkage. Application may have no named executive. Do not count draft as contact or discussion as offer. Segment using honest denominators and show INSUFFICIENT_DATA; six initial preferences cannot establish causal rankings. Protected raw histories stay outside PUBLIC Git.

## Output boundary

Return only cited facts, clearly marked hypotheses and reason-coded HOLDs. Record whether this specialist was natively selected, NOT_VERIFIED, or SKIPPED_NOT_APPLICABLE. Research findings and drafts are not contact-ready or sent.
