# outcome-intelligence

## When to activate
After a daily run or when verified previous engagements or explicit user feedback have changed. Load the private event history through the authorized relationship store, not public Git.

## Procedure
1. Separate discovered, source-corroborated, human-review-approved, contacted, replied, met, introduced and opportunity outcomes. Never infer any stage from generated drafts.
2. Report actual counts and segmented rates using only observed user-recorded events, with denominators and sample sizes; do not conflate a reply with a meeting or a job outcome.
3. Check double counting, selection bias and absent outcome updates; show insufficient sample whenever <10 contacted cases per compared segment.
4. Return factual monthly/weekly activity and observed progress to the daily orchestrator. Never claim improved predictions from a small sample.
5. Never read or copy private email bodies to public repository or execute an external send.
## Output
Source-of-truth event counts, caveats, no contact-status invention, recommendations for human review.

## Organization-first V1.1
Track actual organizational strategy conversation, discussion of a possible role, stated hiring sponsorship, interview and offer separately from researched employer signal, discovered person, message draft or meeting request. A person already known may be connected to a newly developing employer opportunity without becoming a new contact. Keep owner discussion history private.
