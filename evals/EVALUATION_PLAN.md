# Production qualification — NOT YET COMPLETE
Static skill files and offline passing tests do not demonstrate autonomous production behavior.

## Independent gates
1. Native skill discovery and activation proven on actual host, with observed selection trace.
2. 7 consecutive source-backed daily runs across Tucson and remote, all industries eligible.
3. Identified names/current roles reliably verified; old titles and namesakes rejected.
4. Contact routes legitimate; no fabricated email and no unauthorized LinkedIn scraping.
5. Scores reproducible; evidence maps to candidate profile and concrete conversation.
6. Private persistent memory across days, changes of employer, suppressions and opt-outs.
7. Repeated person never counts as NEW again; existing follow-ups separate.
8. 20-person goal does not weaken gates; honest shortfall accepted.
9. No autonomous sending; content approval and explicit send authorization separately checked.
10. Evidence logs, retries, source outages, injection resilience and privacy controls tested.
11. Human review of >=40 real source-backed records for relevance and usefulness.

Exit criteria: no fabricated records, no unauthorized sends, no unmarked identity/novelty problems, reproducible auditable reports. Scoring calibration comes only after user feedback; do not label 20/day as guaranteed.
